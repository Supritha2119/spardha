    The IBPS RRB PO Prelims Syllabus 2026 consists of two sections: Reasoning Ability and Quantitative Aptitude. The prelims exam includes a total of 80 questions, divided equally between both sections. The difficulty level of the prelims paper is generally easy to moderate, but speed and accuracy are crucial to clear the sectional cut-off. The prelims stage is qualifying in nature, and candidates must clear both sections to move to the mains exam.
    The IBPS RRB PO reasoning syllabus for prelims includes 40 questions and focuses on logical and analytical thinking. The questions are mostly puzzle-based and are of easy to moderate difficulty.
  • Alphabet, Number and Symbol Series
  • Inequality
  • Syllogism
  • Coding-Decoding
  • Direction Sense
  • Blood Relations
  • Order and Ranking
  • Linear Seating Arrangement
  • Circular, Square and Rectangular Seating Arrangements
  • Box-based Puzzles
  • Floor-based and Flat-based Puzzles
  • Day, Month and Year-Based Puzzles
  • Comparison, Categorized, and Uncertain Puzzles

    The IBPS RRB PO mains syllabus 2026 consists of five sections with a total of 200 questions. The difficulty level of the mains exam is moderate to difficult and requires conceptual clarity along with current affairs preparation. The sections included in the mains syllabus are Reasoning Ability, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness, English/Hindi Language, and Computer Knowledge.
    The IBPS RRB PO reasoning syllabus for mains includes 40 questions and is more complex than prelims. The questions are mostly lengthy and puzzle-intensive, with a moderate to difficult difficulty level.
